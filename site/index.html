    A NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

    Let the thinking
    take its time.

    This workshop builds from unit squares to finding the area of a shape made from several rectangles. Each lesson follows the same routine: split, measure, multiply, add, and explain.

    • Ask “Which rectangle does that side belong to?” It helps prevent using the whole shape’s length for a smaller piece.
    • Look for gaps and overlaps. Every square inside the shape should belong to exactly one piece.
    • Finish the thought. Add all the piece areas and label the answer with square units.

    The last three lessons reduce the help. An “on my own” check means it was completed without hints, grid help, ruler alignment help, or corrections. A revisit on another day is useful evidence that the idea has stuck.
