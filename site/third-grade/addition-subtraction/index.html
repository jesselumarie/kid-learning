    FOR PARENTS

    Addition and subtraction

    Start with ones and work left. Ten ones equal one ten, ten tens equal one hundred, and ten hundreds equal one thousand. Exchanging blocks changes how a number is grouped, while keeping its value.

    For addition, combine the two groups and exchange any group of ten. For subtraction, exchange a larger block when there are not enough smaller blocks to take away. Numbers with zeros may need several exchanges.

    The first 13 lessons use blocks and aligned answer columns. The last three require your child’s own written calculations. Optional blocks and hints count as help. The app checks answers and operation choices; please review the written calculations on the grade sheet.
