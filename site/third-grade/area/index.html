    FOR PARENTS

    How to help

    The lessons start with counting unit squares and build to finding the area of shapes made from several rectangles. Your child will split shapes, measure sides, multiply, and add.

    • Check each rectangle’s measurements. Ask “Which rectangle does that side belong to?” A smaller piece may not use the whole shape’s length.
    • Check for gaps and overlaps. Every square inside the shape should belong to exactly one piece.
    • Check the final answer. It should include every rectangle’s area and use square units.

    Later lessons start with the grid off. Independent problems record in-app help, including hints, example splits, grid help, ruler alignment, and corrections. Try a problem again another day to check what your child remembers.

    The final problem has a blank scratchpad and a separate answer field. Your child can draw or type calculations. The app checks the final number and units; please review the written work yourself. The grade sheet includes completed results and final work, with a Print / Save PDF button.

    Progress stays in this browser on this device. No accounts, ads, tracking, or uploaded work. The ruler uses model units; it is not a physical screen ruler.
